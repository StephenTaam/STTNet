Chapter 14: Integration and Production

Turn examples into a maintainable, observable service that exits safely under real operational pressure.

1. Integrate with CMake

add_subdirectory(third_party/STTNet)
add_executable(app src/main.cpp)
target_link_libraries(app PRIVATE STTNet::sttnet)

2. Suggested project layout

app/
├── CMakeLists.txt
├── src/
│   ├── main.cpp          # lifecycle and Server construction
│   ├── routes.cpp        # route registration
│   ├── handlers.cpp      # business processing
│   └── shutdown.cpp      # signals and control thread
├── config/
└── third_party/STTNet/

3. Production checklist

  • Compiler, C++17 ABI, and dependencies are pinned and reproducible.
  • ulimit -n, systemd LimitNOFILE, and container limits match connection targets.
  • Every database, RPC, filesystem, and lock wait has a finite timeout.
  • Worker and write queues are bounded and rejection/overflow metrics are monitored.
  • TLS certificates cover real hostnames; private-key permissions are minimal; verification is enabled.
  • SIGTERM exercises graceful shutdown and forced convergence after the configured deadline.
  • Security rate limits are tuned for real traffic and benchmark environments are explicit.
  • Logs rotate and do not contain credentials, tokens, or full sensitive payloads.
  • Health checks are cheap and do not depend on unstable external systems.
  • Load tests include keep-alive, slow clients, large responses, and Worker pressure—not only an empty /ping.

4. Describe performance honestly

A historical 65k QPS / 2–3 ms result belongs to a specific hardware, kernel, connection model, and workload. Publish the environment and method; do not promise the same number for every machine or business callback.
